To vote, a citizen must be 18 years of age or older as of January 1 of the election year.
The voter must possess a valid EPIC (Electors Photo Identity Card) or Voter ID.
If the Voter ID is misplaced, alternative IDs like Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Driving License are accepted at the polling booth, provided the voter's name is on the official Electoral Roll.
